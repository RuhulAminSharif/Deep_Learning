## DL vs ML  

1. **Data Dependency**:  
   - **Machine Learning (ML)**: Performs well on smaller datasets, as many algorithms are effective with limited data.  
   - **Deep Learning (DL)**: Requires large volumes of data to achieve high performance due to its reliance on layered neural networks.  

2. **Hardware Dependency**:  
   - **ML**: Can run efficiently on standard CPUs and doesn't necessarily require high computational power.  
   - **DL**: Requires specialized hardware like GPUs or TPUs to handle complex computations and large datasets.  

3. **Training Time**:  
   - **ML**: Generally faster to train since it deals with fewer parameters and simpler models.  
   - **DL**: Training can be time-consuming due to the extensive computations and numerous parameters involved in neural networks.  

4. **Feature Selection**:  
   - **ML**: Requires manual feature extraction and engineering, where domain expertise is often crucial.  
   - **DL**: Automatically learns features from raw data, minimizing the need for manual intervention.  

5. **Interpretability**:  
   - **ML**: Models like decision trees or linear regression are more interpretable, making it easier to understand predictions.  
   - **DL**: Acts like a "black box," making it harder to interpret and explain model decisions.
